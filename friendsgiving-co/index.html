How it works

Three steps to your free Friendsgiving sessions

  1. 1Choose your studio below and tap Book a class.
  2. 2Sign in to Mindbody, or create a free account. It takes a minute and includes the studio waiver.
  3. 3Reserve any class from Nov 16 to 22, then show up and train with us.

Before you come in

A few ground rules

  • Book every class in advance on Mindbody. We can’t take walk-ins this week.
  • Arrive at least 5 minutes early to keep your reservation. Late spots may be released to the waitlist.
  • Classes may fill up. Interest is high, so book early and cancel in Mindbody if your plans change so someone else can take your spot.
  • Sign the waiver before your first class. It’s part of your Mindbody sign-up.
