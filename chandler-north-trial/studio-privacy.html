Studio Privacy Notice

This page describes what the Chandler North trial signup page collects and how it is used.

What we collect

When you submit the trial form, we collect your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and ZIP code. When you visit the page, the Meta Pixel may collect page-view and lead-event information as described below.

How we use it

  • To create your Mindbody account so you can book your trial classes.
  • To contact you about your trial by email and phone.
  • To send automated text messages, only if you checked the text consent box.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who processes it

  • Mindbody (via Rota.fit / Zapier) — to create your client account.
  • Stripe — to process your $30 payment. Card details are entered on Stripe's checkout page, not on this page.
  • Meta — the Meta Pixel records PageView and Lead events so we can measure how the signup page performs.

Text messages

If you check the text consent box on the signup form, F45 Training Chandler North may send you recurring automated marketing and trial-related text messages (for example, booking reminders, class follow-ups and offers) at the mobile number you provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help, or contact the studio using the details below.
  • Texts are sent through our messaging providers (Quo and HighLevel).
  • We do not share or sell your mobile number or text messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We record whether you checked the box, the wording you agreed to, the date and time, and the page you signed up on.

Your choices

To ask about, correct, or delete the details you submitted, contact the studio at 1949 W Ray Rd, Unit 33, Chandler, AZ 85224, or by phone at 480-716-2682.
